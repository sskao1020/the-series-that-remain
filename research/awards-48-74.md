## Data-model notes
- Keep `organization`, `awardYear`, `category`, `recipient`, `result`, `sourceUrl`, and optional `ceremonyDate` at record level.
- Keep organization-level `nominationCount` and `winCount` separately; never derive “nominations” by adding wins unless the source explicitly defines nominations as all finalist citations (the Television Academy title pages do).
- Store Honors separately from competitive Emmys (`awardKind: honor`), or win-rate calculations will be misleading.
- Use a stable `workId`; do not silently combine revivals, reunion specials, or finale films with the original series. Frasier’s current Television Academy total is a concrete reason for this rule.
