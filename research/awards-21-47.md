# Awards research: series 21–47

Research date: 2026-08-10

## Scope and data notes

- These records prioritize first-party award bodies. `wins` and `nominations` are organization-specific totals, not an all-awards aggregate.
- A Television Academy page's “Nominations” count includes both winning and non-winning entries; `wins` is the separately displayed Emmy count. A missing Emmy count means zero wins.
- Representative records are deliberately selective (1–4 per series). `result` is always explicit so the data can later support win-rate calculations.
- Ceremony date is omitted unless it is needed to distinguish an award year from a delayed ceremony. Recipients are included where the official record names them.
- Official URLs should be retained in the site database even if the public UI does not expose a “view full awards data” link.

## Verification cautions for implementation

1. Do not sum totals across organizations and call the result a universal “award total”; eligibility and counting rules differ.
2. Preserve zero wins explicitly. *Better Call Saul*, *The Good Place*, *The Leftovers*, *BoJack Horseman*, *Narcos*, and *The Underground Railroad* are useful future “low award conversion / high canon value” cases.
3. Keep anthology-film records separate for *Black Mirror* to avoid undercounting its historic Emmy wins.
4. The Television Academy currently combines *Deadwood* and *Deadwood: The Movie* on one historical page; retain that scope note if using its 36/8 totals.
